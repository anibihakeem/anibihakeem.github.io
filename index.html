About

Most of my work sits between the database and the decision. I write the SQL and the pipelines, build the semantic model so business logic lives in one governed place, and check that the numbers hold before anyone presents them. Month-end validation and reconciliation work taught me that a report nobody trusts changes nothing.

I started as a project analyst, building cost and margin dashboards for project leadership, and moved into full-time data work from there. Since then I have delivered reporting and automation for teams in logistics, FMCG, professional services and research, as an employee, a consultant and a freelancer. Alongside that work I teach SQL, through published tutorials, recorded webinars and an online course.

I am now completing an MSc in Data Science at Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II, which has taken me further into machine learning, streaming systems and distributed data. This year my modules cover AI systems engineering, methods for AI and algorithm design, information systems and BI, and data security. I want work where both halves count.

I like learning new things, and most of what I can do today I picked up because a problem needed it.
